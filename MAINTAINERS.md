# Maintainers

The people below can review and merge pull requests, cut releases, and
administer this repository. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for what that
means in practice and how the list changes.

All of them are members of the `@Neosmith-ai/maintainers` team, which is what
[`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS) points at. Adding someone here
without adding them to that team has no effect.

## Emeritus

_None yet._

Maintainers inactive for six months move here and have their repository access
removed. It is reversible on request — see
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#becoming-a-maintainer).

## Contacting us

- **A bug or a feature request** → [open an issue](https://github.com/Neosmith-ai/neosmith-connect/issues/new/choose).
- **A security problem** → do *not* open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else** → <contact-us@neosmith.ai>.
